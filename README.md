# ⚡ Static Shock

## Introduction

Static Shock is a superhero from DC Comics. His real name is Virgil Hawkins, and he lives in Dakota City. He gets his powers after being exposed to a chemical explosion called the Big Bang.

Virgil is not a typical superhero. He is still a teenager, so he has to deal with school, friends and family while also fighting criminals. I think this makes him an interesting character.

## ⚡ Powers and Abilities

Static has different powers connected to electricity and magnetism.

- **Electricity:** He can create and control electricity.
- **Magnetism:** He can move metal objects using magnetic forces.
- **Flying:** He uses a metal disc to fly around the city.
- **Electric Attacks:** He can shoot electricity at his enemies.
- **Technology:** He is good at science and understands how technology works.

One of my favourite things about Static is that he uses his powers in creative ways instead of just attacking his enemies.

## 🦸 His Story

### From Student to Superhero

Before getting his powers, Virgil was just a normal student. After the Big Bang, his life changed completely.

He decided to use his new abilities to help people and protect his city. Even though he has superpowers, he still makes mistakes and sometimes gets into difficult situations.

**What makes Static interesting is that he is both a normal teenager and a superhero.**

## 🔋 How Do His Powers Work?

Static's abilities are connected to electromagnetism. He can control electrical energy and use magnetic forces to move objects.

Inline code example: `static_attack(100)`

```python
def static_attack(power):
    if power <= 0:
```
